# Reflection

## What was easy
Using PartyRock’s Whiskers to add blocks step by step was simple and intuitive.

## What was hard
Fixing placeholder references and making sure the prompts worked correctly took extra effort.

## What I would change
Next time, I’d plan the app flow first and then add widgets in order, to avoid confusion.
